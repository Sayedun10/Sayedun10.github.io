HubSpot Service Hub pricing, explained

Since March 2024, HubSpot has charged new customers per seat. You pay for each person who works in the help desk, and people who only need to look are free on view-only seats.

Three ways to keep the bill down

  • Only give paid seats to people who work tickets. Managers and other teams who only need to look can use free view-only seats.
  • Compare annual and monthly billing. Annual billing is cheaper per seat, but you're committed for the year. Monthly billing makes sense while your team size is still changing.
  • Budget for onboarding. Professional and Enterprise have a required one-time fee, so in the first year a small team can pay more in onboarding than in seats.

Need to know where support hours go?

Service Hub doesn't track how long tickets take. Tally Time adds automatic time tracking to HubSpot tickets and shows retainer hours on each company. It works on any Service Hub plan, and it's free to install during beta.
