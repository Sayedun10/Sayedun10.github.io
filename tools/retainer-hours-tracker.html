Tracking client hours in HubSpot?

Tally Time does this automatically. It logs time while your team works on HubSpot tickets, shows "38 of 50 hours used" on each company record, and warns you at 80%. No timers to remember.

How to use this tracker

  1. Add a client with their monthly retainer hours. If unused hours carry over, enter last month's leftover.
  2. Log each piece of work with a date, a short description and the hours, in quarter hours if you like (0.25 is 15 minutes).
  3. Pick the month at the top to see that month's hours. Each month starts from zero.
  4. Watch the bar. It turns amber at 80% and red when you go over. If you set an overage rate, the tracker shows what you can bill.
  5. At month end, click Print client report and save it as a PDF to send. Click Download CSV to open your data in Google Sheets or Excel.

Your data is saved in this browser only. Clearing your browser data, or opening the page on another device, starts fresh, so download the CSV to keep a copy.
