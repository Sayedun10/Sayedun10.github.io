Client hours report email templates

Two short emails every retainer client should get: the 80% heads-up and the monthly hours summary. Copy them, then replace everything in [brackets].

1. The 80% heads-up

Send this when a client reaches 80% of the month's hours. It gives them a choice before the hours run out, instead of a surprise invoice afterwards.

Subject: Heads-up: [80]% of your [Month] hours used

Hi [First name],

Quick heads-up: we've used [16] of your [20] retainer hours for [Month].

The remaining [4] hours will cover:
- [Monthly report]
- [Two open support requests]

If anything else comes up before [month end date], we can:
1. Hold it until next month, when your hours reset, or
2. Go ahead now at [$X] per additional hour.

Just reply with which you'd prefer. No action is needed if you're happy for us to hold new requests until next month.

Thanks,
[Your name]

2. The monthly hours summary

Send this in the first few days of each month. Clients who can see the work renew more often and question invoices less.

Subject: [Client name] support hours for [Month]

Hi [First name],

Here's your support summary for [Month].

Hours used: [16.5] of [20] ([83]%)
Hours carried over from last month: [0]
Hours remaining: [3.5] (these [expire / carry over] at month end)

What we worked on:
- [Date]: [Updated the pricing page] ([1.5] h)
- [Date]: [Monthly analytics report] ([3] h)
- [Date]: [Fixed contact form notifications] ([2] h)
- [Date]: [Support tickets, 9 resolved] ([10] h)

Coming up next month:
- [Planned item 1]
- [Planned item 2]

[Any overage: We went [2] hours over this month, which we agreed on [date]. These are billed at [$X] per hour on your next invoice.]

Let me know if you have any questions or want to change priorities for next month.

Thanks,
[Your name]
