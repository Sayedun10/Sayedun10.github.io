Retainer agreement template: hours, rollover and overage

A sample clause for monthly hourly retainers. It covers what the hours include, how time is tracked, the 80% usage alert, rollover and overage billing. Replace everything in [brackets] and pick one option for unused hours.

Monthly Support Retainer: Hours, Rollover and Overage

1. Retainer hours
[Agency name] will provide up to [20] hours of [support / maintenance / marketing] services each calendar month for a fixed fee of [$X] per month, invoiced [in advance on the 1st of each month].

2. What the hours cover
Included: [support requests, minor content and site updates, monthly reporting, client calls].
Not included: [new projects, redesigns, third-party costs such as software or ad spend, rush work requested with less than 24 hours' notice]. These are quoted separately.

3. How time is tracked
Time is tracked in [15]-minute increments. Each month we share a report listing the date, a description and the time for each piece of work.

4. Usage alerts
We will notify [client contact] when [80]% of the month's hours have been used, so we can agree on priorities for the rest of the month.

5. Unused hours
[Choose one]
(a) Unused hours expire at the end of each month and do not carry over.
(b) Up to [5] unused hours carry over to the next month only, then expire.

6. Additional hours
Work beyond the monthly hours needs the client's approval by email. Approved additional hours are billed at [$X] per hour and invoiced with the following month's retainer.

7. Changes and cancellation
Either party may change the number of retainer hours or cancel this retainer with [30] days' written notice.

This is a sample clause for illustration. Have a lawyer review your agreement before you use it.

This is an example, not legal advice. Retainer terms vary by country and industry, so have a lawyer review your agreement.
