05Questions
Doesn't HubSpot already track time to close?

Time to close measures how long a ticket stayed open, including nights, weekends and time waiting on the customer. It doesn't tell you how much work the ticket took. Tally Time measures the work.

Which HubSpot plans does Tally Time work with?

Any HubSpot account that uses tickets. The setup guide has the details.

What does it cost?

It's free during the beta, with a free plan for up to 3 agents after that. Paid plans are on the pricing page.

Are the free tools really free?

Yes. They run in your browser, need no account, and your data stays on your device.
