Privacy policy

Last updated November 26, 2026. This policy covers Tally Time, a HubSpot app made by Northpine Apps.

What we store

  • Your HubSpot account ID, and the HubSpot user IDs of people who use the app.
  • The IDs of tickets and companies where time is tracked. We store IDs only, not names or content.
  • Time sessions: when each started, how long it ran, and the minutes confirmed or discarded.
  • Retainer hours you set for each company.
  • The access tokens HubSpot gives the app when you install it.

What we do not access

Tally Time does not read ticket text, emails, conversations, contacts, or deal information. The only thing it writes to HubSpot is one number field on tickets, "Tally Time hours."

How we use it

Only to run the app: to show time and retainer usage in your HubSpot account and to keep the ticket field up to date. We don't sell or share your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

On Cloudflare's servers, kept separately for each HubSpot account.

Deleting your data

You can uninstall the app from HubSpot at any time. To have your stored data deleted, email vicedexinfo@gmail.com from an address on the account. We delete it within 30 days.
