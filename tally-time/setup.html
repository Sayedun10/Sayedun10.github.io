Tally Time setup guide

Tally Time tracks support time on HubSpot tickets automatically and shows retainer hours on companies. Setup takes about five minutes.

1. Install the app

Install Tally Time free (beta)

Click the button above and pick your HubSpot account. HubSpot may say the app is unverified, which is normal for apps that are not on the Marketplace yet. Approve the permissions. The app needs to read tickets and companies, write one ticket property, and create that property.

When the install finishes, Tally Time adds a number property to tickets called Tally Time hours.

2. Add the ticket card

  1. In HubSpot, click the settings icon in the top bar.
  2. In the left menu, under Data Management, click Objects, then choose Tickets.
  3. Open the Record Customization tab and click Default view.
  4. In the right sidebar, click the + icon, open the Card library, find Tally Time, and click Add card.
  5. Click Save, then Save and exit.

3. Add the retainer card (optional)

If you sell support retainers, repeat step 2 for Companies and add the Tally Time retainer card.

How tracking works

  • Opening a ticket starts the timer. It counts while the ticket stays open, even if the browser tab is in the background.
  • Reloading the page within five minutes continues the same session.
  • Closing the ticket, or about two and a half minutes without contact, ends the session. Click Stop and review to end it yourself.
  • Finished sessions show under To review. Adjust the minutes if needed, then click Confirm or Discard.
  • Confirmed time is added to the ticket's Tally Time hours property, so you can use it in HubSpot reports, views and workflows.

Retainers

  • On a company, enter the monthly retainer hours and click Save.
  • The card shows hours used this month from confirmed time on tickets associated with that company. It warns at 80% and again at 100%.
  • Click Copy client report to copy a summary by ticket that you can paste into an email or invoice.
  • Months follow UTC.

Uninstalling

Uninstall the app from HubSpot's Connected apps page. The Tally Time hours property stays on your tickets until you delete it. To have the data we store deleted, see our privacy policy.
